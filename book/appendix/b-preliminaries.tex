\chapter{Preliminaries and Notation}
\label{app:preliminaries}

This appendix fixes notation and nothing else. It is not a course in
mathematics: the reader is assumed to have the general mathematical maturity of
a bachelor's degree in computer science or mathematics, and standard material
--- sets, functions, induction, elementary probability --- is used without
comment. What is collected here is only the notation this book uses in a way
that might differ from another book's, and the small number of specific facts
that several chapters lean on.
