\chapter[Exact Algorithms II: Inclusion--Exclusion]{Exact Exponential-Time Algorithms II: Subsets, Inclusion--Exclusion, and Lower Bounds}
\label{ch:exact-ii}

Branching is not the only way to spend an exponential budget. Dynamic
programming over subsets gives $\Ostar(2^n)$ for problems where the obvious
algorithm is $n!$, and inclusion--exclusion often gives the same running time in
polynomial space. We close by asking how much better one could hope to do, which
is where the Exponential Time Hypothesis comes in.

\begin{goals}
  \poolgoal{design dynamic programming algorithms over subsets and analyse their time and space}
  \poolgoal{solve \lang{TSP} in $\Ostar(2^n)$ time by the Held--Karp algorithm}
  \poolgoal{state the inclusion--exclusion principle and use it to trade space for time}
  \poolgoal{give an $\Ostar(2^m)$ time, polynomial-space algorithm that counts the number of minimum set covers}
  \poolgoal{state ETH and SETH and derive conditional lower bounds from them}
\end{goals}
