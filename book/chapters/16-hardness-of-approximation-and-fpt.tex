\chapter[Hardness of Approximation and Parameterization]{Complexity Theory for Approximation and Parameterization}
\label{ch:hardness}

Both of the previous parts leave an obvious question open: which problems admit
a PTAS, and which problems are FPT? This chapter provides the negative theory.
For approximation the key notions are APX-hardness and the L-reduction; for
parameterization they are the W-hierarchy and ETH-based lower bounds.

\begin{goals}
  \goalitem{define APX and approximation-preserving reductions}
  \goalitem{define an L-reduction and prove that L-reductions compose and preserve constant-factor approximability}
  \poolgoal{18}{prove that \lang{Max Cut on Multigraphs} is APX-hard by giving an L-reduction from \lang{Max Not-All-Equal 3-SAT}}
  \goalitem{state the PCP theorem and explain how it yields inapproximability results}
  \goalitem{define the W-hierarchy and W[1]-hardness, and give W[1]-hard problems}
  \goalitem{derive parameterized lower bounds from ETH}
\end{goals}
